As required by the course AI policy, this appendix lists my prompts, what the AI produced, and the edits I made. A per-question log is also in the repository (\texttt{ai\_log.md}). All training and evaluation runs were executed by me on Kaggle; the AI never ran my real experiments (it only tested notebooks on synthetic data).
